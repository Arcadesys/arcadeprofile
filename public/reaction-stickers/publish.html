Make it a Telegram set.

Bring ten transparent PNGs from your own chat, sign in with Telegram, and publish a set you own.

Images go to Telegram only after you review this form and press Publish. This page does not generate stickers.

Checking Telegram publishing…

1. Sign in with Telegram

Telegram confirms which account will own the set. Use the account where you want the stickers to appear.

Sign in with Telegram

Start @stickerslopbot in Telegram first so it can publish your set.

2. Name your set

Lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores. The final Telegram name also includes “_by_stickerslopbot”.

3. Choose your ten PNGs

These slots show the standard reaction order. If you made custom reactions, use the same positions and change their emoji. One side of each transparent PNG must be exactly 512 pixels; each file must be 512 KB or less. The whole upload must fit within 3.8 MB.

4. Review and publish

Choose all ten PNGs to review the pack.

Publishing copies these images into a shareable Telegram sticker set owned by your signed-in account. This demo allows up to three upload attempts per account each day.

The PNGs are sent to this site only when you publish. They are forwarded to Telegram and are not stored by this site. Telegram account verification uses @stickerslopbot; no bot token is entered here.
