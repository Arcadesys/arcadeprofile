THE FIVE RULES

FOR THE WORK AHEAD

Based on Robert A. Heinlein’s rules for writers. Read Heinlein’s essay. The sections below present Heinlein Studio’s interpretation.

  1. 01You must write.Begin the work.
  2. 02You must finish what you write.Bring it to done.
  3. 03You must refrain from rewriting except to editorial order.Trust your choices.
  4. 04You must put it on the market.Prepare the work for readers.
  5. 05You must keep it on the market until it is sold.Give it a chance to be found.
01

The story starts here.

Open your manuscript. Find your scene. Put down the next word.

02

Done is something you make.

A finished story doesn’t happen all at once. Find what’s missing, work through the rough edges, and bring each part closer to the whole.

When you want another perspective, AI assistance can help you explore a problem, smooth a passage, or find the spark a scene needs. You decide what belongs.

A modern interpretation of finishing, informed by lean and agile ways of getting work to done.

03

Your voice belongs to you.

Review when you choose. Consider the feedback. Make the call yourself.

04

Make something readers can open.

Turn your manuscript into editions for print, ebook, and the web.

05

Give the story its chance.

Make the editions. Put them to work. Keep going.

Five rules. One place to begin.
