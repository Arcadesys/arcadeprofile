*by Jumpin’ Jack Rabbit (1973–2040)*

It takes a Zoo to raise a child,\
To keep their heart both soft and wild.\
It takes a village, so they say,\
To hold the dark and point the way.

It takes a Zoo to make them room,\
To let each strange, bright spirit bloom.\
To guide them through the storm and flame,\
And teach them love can know their name.

A Zoo can raise its own ones too;\
Each grown-up soul was once born new.\
Within these walls, we learn to stand,\
And trust the pulse beneath our hands.

So lift your cups and close your eyes,\
A toast to every child who tries:\
That in this world, whatever they live through,\
They’ll always have a home within the Zoo.
